# Rover Villa

**rovervilla.space** — a memorial for the machines we left behind on Mars and the Moon.

> Their mission ended. Their story didn't.

## The idea

Rovers, landers and probes went where we couldn't, and they never came back. Their missions are over, but they are still out there. We remember them the way we remember great people: where they were born, the life they lived, what they discovered, and their last words.

## Who we feature

Retired or lost space hardware on **Mars** (Viking, Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Phoenix, InSight, Ingenuity…) and the **Moon** (Ranger, Surveyor, Apollo landers and rovers, LCROSS, GRAIL…).

## Each hero's page

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Name, nickname, catalogue no., born / last signal dates, resting place, key stats |
| **Documentary** | AI-narrated video: the machine tells its own story, first person, built only from real mission facts |
| **Born** | Lab and assembly photos, the team who built it, specs |
| **3D model** | Interactive model with labelled parts |
| **Launch day** | Date, rocket, launch pad |
| **Life's work** | Landing, chapters of the mission, route map, discoveries |
| **Last signal** | Final transmission date and the story of the goodbye |
| **Gallery** | Photos, videos, raw images |
| **Tribute wall** | Visitors leave messages |

## Three ways to explore

1. **Standard** (default): browse heroes, the map and the gallery.
2. **Story**: a scroll-driven, cinematic journey through each hero's life, chapter by chapter.
3. **Timeline**: every hero on one axis, from Ranger 7 (1964) to today.

Plus **Where They Rest**: an interactive Mars/Moon globe showing each landing site.

## Look and feel

- Clean, bold, modern: white base, deep ink text, **one accent colour: electric violet `#5b2eff`**, with Mars orange `#ff5a1f` as a tiny accent only.
- Ultra-bold display type plus mono labels for dates and data.
- Multi-column, editorial "exhibit card" layout with catalogue numbers (`No. 07`).
- A signal waveform is the recurring motif.
- Web and mobile app designs are in this folder (`v3-vibrant/`).

## Ground rules

- **Facts only.** Every date, number and "last words" line must come from a real source. No invented quotes.
- **Real imagery.** Use NASA's public-domain photos and videos, credited. The AI images in the mockups are placeholders.
- **No NASA branding.** No NASA logo or "NASA" in our name, and nothing that implies an official endorsement.
- **AI is labelled.** Any AI narration or video is clearly marked as AI-made.
